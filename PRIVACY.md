# Nothing Island privacy policy

Nothing Island processes information on the device to provide its visible island overlay.

## Information used

- Notification titles and short text are read through Android's user-granted notification listener access and shown in the island.
- Active media session title and artist are read while a media session is playing and shown in the island.
- Charging state and battery percentage are read to show charging status.

## Storage and sharing

The app does not send this information to a server, save it to app storage, share it with third parties, or use analytics/advertising SDKs. Notification and media text is held in memory only while displayed. The app declares no Internet permission.

## Permissions and choices

Notification access and display-over-other-apps access are optional Android special permissions. The app explains both before sending the user to Android Settings. Declining keeps the related feature off. Either permission can be revoked in Android Settings at any time. The app does not use the Accessibility Service API, inspect screen contents, control other apps, or dismiss notifications.

## Contact

For a public release, the app publisher should add a support contact and host this policy at a public URL linked from the store listing.
